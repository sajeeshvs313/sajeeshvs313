FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What materials and processes does PrecisionWorks work with?

We machine steel, stainless steel, aluminium, brass and engineering plastics using CNC turning and milling, and also offer fabrication and sheet metal work.

What tolerances can you achieve?

Our CNC machines can hold tolerances up to ±0.01 mm depending on the material, size and drawing (sample specification).

How fast can I get a quote?

We provide a quotation within 24 hours of receiving a drawing or sample.

Do you accept small prototype orders?

Yes. We handle prototypes from a single piece up to full production batches.

Are you ISO certified?

Yes. PrecisionWorks operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system with documented inspection.
