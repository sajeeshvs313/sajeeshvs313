FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a bookkeeper do for a small business?

A bookkeeper records and categorises your transactions, reconciles your bank and card accounts and produces monthly reports such as a profit and loss statement and a balance sheet, so you always know where the business stands.

How much does bookkeeping cost for a small business?

Fees depend on transaction volume and complexity. In this sample, monthly bookkeeping starts at $299. A real firm would agree the fee in writing before starting.

Do you file taxes?

No. LedgerLane provides bookkeeping, not tax preparation or tax advice. We keep your books clean and share notes with your CPA, who handles tax returns.

Which software do you use?

We work in QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you use something else, tell us on the intro call and we will check whether we can support it.

Can you catch up on past months?

Yes. A cleanup project reviews past records, fixes reconciliations and prepares notes for your CPA. We give a fixed quote after reviewing your books.
