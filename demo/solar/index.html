FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a rooftop solar system cost in Kochi?

Rooftop solar in Kochi costs about ₹60,000 per kW before subsidy, so a 3 kW home system starts around ₹1,80,000 and a 5 kW system around ₹2,90,000 (sample prices).

Is there a government subsidy for rooftop solar?

Residential rooftop solar can qualify for central subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. We help with the application and paperwork.

How much can I save on my electricity bill?

Most homes cut their electricity bill by 70 to 90 percent, with payback in about 3 to 4 years, depending on usage and system size.

How long does solar installation take?

A typical home installation takes 5 to 7 days after the design and approvals are complete.

What warranty do the panels carry?

Panels typically carry a 25-year performance warranty and inverters 5 to 10 years. We also offer annual maintenance.
