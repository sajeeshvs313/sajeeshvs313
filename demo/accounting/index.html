Frequently asked questions

How much does GST registration cost in Kochi?

GST registration at Ledger & Co. starts at ₹1,499 as a one-time fee (sample price). The GSTIN is usually issued in 3 to 7 working days.

What documents are needed to register a company?

You need PAN and Aadhaar of directors, address proof, passport photos and proof of the registered office. We guide you step by step.

Can you file income tax returns for individuals?

Yes. We file ITR for salaried individuals, professionals, business owners and NRIs, starting at ₹999.

Do you offer monthly accounting?

Yes. Monthly bookkeeping packages start at ₹3,999 and include GST returns and MIS reports.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first business consultation is free, so you can understand the process and cost before deciding.
