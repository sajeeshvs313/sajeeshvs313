✦ What your website needs

Six things a crane service website must do well

1

Fleet and capacities

Each crane type with tonnage, reach and typical use, so buyers can match it to their lift.

2

Rate guidance

Starting hourly or daily rates, with a note on what changes the final price.

3

Safety and credentials

Operator certification, insurance and inspection information, shown without exaggeration.

4

Quote request with lift details

A form that asks weight, height, radius and location and opens WhatsApp.

5

Service area

The districts you cover and response time for emergencies.

6

Past jobs

Short descriptions of lifts you have completed, with permission.

✦ Common mistakes

Mistakes we see, and how to fix them

Only a phone number

Buyers compare several suppliers. A quote form that captures the lift details gets answered faster.

No tonnage information

Visitors cannot tell if you can handle their job. List capacities clearly.

Unverified safety claims

Only state certifications and insurance you actually hold, and keep documents ready.
