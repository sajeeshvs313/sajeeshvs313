✦ What your website needs

Six things a auditorium website must do well

1

Capacity and layouts

Seating for theatre, banquet and standing formats, with hall dimensions.

2

Photos and a virtual walk-through

Real images of the hall, stage, dining area and parking.

3

Rent guidance

Starting rates by event type and duration, with what is included.

4

Date check enquiry

A form that captures event type, date and guests and opens WhatsApp.

5

Facilities list

AC, sound, lights, green rooms, generator and parking in a simple list.

6

Catering and decoration policy

Clarify in-house and outside options to avoid last-minute disputes.

✦ Common mistakes

Mistakes we see, and how to fix them

No capacity numbers

Planners cannot shortlist without them. State seated and standing capacity.

Only a phone number for bookings

People enquire in the evening. A form that captures dates works around the clock.

Hiding parking details

Parking is often the deciding factor. Give numbers and access information.

✦ FAQ

Questions auditoriums & event venues ask us

How much does an auditorium website cost in Kerala?

An auditorium website with hall details, a gallery and a date-enquiry form typically costs ₹12,000 with Qrenzy, and a one-page version starts at ₹5,000. First-year domain and hosting are included.

Can organisers check dates on the website?

They can send a date enquiry that opens WhatsApp with event type, date and guest count. We do not show a live calendar unless you ask for a custom option.

What should an event venue page include?

Capacity, layouts, photos, facilities, parking, catering policy, indicative rent and a clear way to enquire.

Does a venue need a Google Business Profile as well?

Yes. Many organisers search on Google Maps first, so a complete profile with photos and reviews complements the website.
