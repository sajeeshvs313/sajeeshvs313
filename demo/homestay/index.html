FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a room cost at Green Valley Homestay in Munnar?

Rooms start at ₹2,200 per night with breakfast included. Family suites are ₹3,800 and the entire cottage is ₹6,500 (sample rates).

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes. A home-cooked Kerala breakfast is included. Lunch and dinner can be arranged on request.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Early check-in is possible when rooms are free.

How do I get a direct booking discount?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates. Direct bookings get 10% off and a free welcome drink.

Do you arrange pickup and sightseeing?

Yes. We can arrange pickup from Munnar town and local sightseeing with trusted drivers.
