FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the tuition fees at BrightPath?

Fees start at ₹800 per month for Classes 5 to 7, ₹1,200 for SSLC and ₹1,800 for Plus One and Two (sample fees).

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. New students can attend one demo class free before deciding to join.

How many students are in each batch?

Each batch has a maximum of 20 students so every child gets personal attention.

Do you coach for KEAM and NEET?

Yes. Entrance coaching for KEAM, NEET and other exams runs as weekend and crash-course batches.

How will I know my child's progress?

We share weekly test marks and monthly progress reports with parents on WhatsApp.
