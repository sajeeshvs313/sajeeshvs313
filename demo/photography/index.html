FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Kochi?

Wedding photography in Kochi at Frame & Light starts around ₹65,000 for a full-day photo and video package, with essential event coverage from ₹15,000 (sample prices).

How soon do we get the photos?

You get a preview gallery in about 3 days and the full edited gallery in around 10 days. Albums take about 30 days.

Do you travel outside Kochi?

Yes. We cover weddings and shoots across Kerala and beyond. Travel costs are added to the quote.

Do you offer drone and video coverage?

Yes. Packages can include 4K video, cinematic highlight films and drone shots where permitted.

How do I confirm my date?

Share your date on WhatsApp, and pay a small advance to block it. Dates are booked on a first-come basis.
