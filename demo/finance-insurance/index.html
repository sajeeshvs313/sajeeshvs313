FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much life insurance cover do I need?

A common guide is 10 to 15 times your annual income, adjusted for loans and goals. We calculate this with you during a free review.

What is the minimum amount to start a SIP?

You can start a mutual fund SIP from ₹500 a month with most fund houses.

Is your advice free?

Yes. Policy reviews and planning are free. We are paid by the insurers and fund houses for products you choose, and we disclose this clearly.

Can you help with claim settlement?

Yes. We guide you through documentation and follow up with the insurer to support your claim.

Do you help with home and business loans?

Yes. We help compare lenders, prepare documents and track approvals for home and business loans.
